Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Policy explains how Nasib Fahim Fernández (Spain) (the “Developer”, “we”) handles information when you use RepTr: Gym progress tracker (the “App”), a gym training log. We comply with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and other applicable data protection laws.

In short: RepTr does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Developer and data controller
Nasib Fahim Fernández (España / Spain)

Contact
reptr.app.contact@gmail.com

1. Data stored on your device

The App saves the following only in its private storage on your phone. We never receive it.

CategoryDataSource
TrainingRoutines, exercises, sets, reps, weights and the dates you log themEntered by you
SessionsStart time and duration of the workouts you timeGenerated by the App
SettingsWork and rest seconds of each routine, timer volume and the session in progressChosen by you

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, account, location, photos, contacts, device identifiers, advertising ID, analytics or crash reports. The App does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.

3. Android backup

If Android backup is turned on on your device, Android may include the App's data in your personal backup, stored encrypted in your own Google account, so it can be restored when you reinstall the App or change phones. This backup is managed by Google under your account settings and we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device settings.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhat it is for
NotificationsShow the workout timer (phase, time, pause and finish buttons) while you train
Foreground service and keep awakeKeep the interval timer counting and alerting you with the screen locked, only during a workout you start
VibrationAlert you when a work or rest phase starts

The App does not request access to the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

5. Purchase

The App is sold through Google Play. Payment is processed entirely by Google under its own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive your card or payment details; Google only provides us with sales reports and order identifiers.

6. Third parties

The App contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or social media SDKs, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App, which removes it from the device. Data in your Android backup is kept according to your Google account settings.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold any of your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side: you control all your data directly in the App. You can still contact us with any question and, if you are in the EU, lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD: www.aepd.es).

9. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Changes

If we change this Policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date.
